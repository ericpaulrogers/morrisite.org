On the roll

Karin Caroline Eliasson (1830–1902)

A Swedish-born Morrisite, wife of Erik Eliasson, who came to Soda Springs in 1863 and joined the RLDS Church in 1870.

On the roll
Eliason, Karen, in the 1861–62 roll of membership printed in 1886
Born
March 1830, Sweden (Family Tree)
Died
1902, Idaho (Family Tree)
Later path
Stayed Morrisite, later joined the RLDS Church. Lived at Soda Springs with the Morrisites after 1862, where her son John Eric was born in 1867 (family account, Keith Chappelow, 2026); baptized into the RLDS Church on June 9, 1870, as Caroline Eliasson, born March 7, 1830, in Sweden (Black, Early Members of the Reorganized Church, vol. 2, s.v. "Eliasson, Caroline").

What the sources say

Karin Caroline Anderson (1830–1902), wife of Erik Eliasson and mother of John Eric Eliason, born at Soda Springs in 1867 (FamilySearch LHN8-4C5). Shupe lists Mrs. Caroline Eliason among the Morrisites who came to Soda Springs with General Connor in May 1863 (Shupe, Caribou County Chronology, p. 13). Baptized into the RLDS Church with her husband on June 9, 1870, as Caroline Eliasson (Black, Early Members of the Reorganized Church, vol. 2). Identified by her descendant Keith Chappelow.
