William Nutter (1830–1906)

What the sources say

William Nutter (1830–1906), born in England, who came to Utah with his wife Dinah in the John Smith Company of 1860 (Church History Biographical Database; FamilySearch KWJW-4FB); the roll spells the name Nuttor. They later settled in Nebraska, where he died at Shelton, Buffalo County, in 1906.
