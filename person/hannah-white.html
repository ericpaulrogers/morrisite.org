What the sources say

Hannah White (1844–1868), born in England, daughter of William and Rhoda White and sister of William J. White and Sarah Harris, all on the roll with their mother as Rhoda Jones; she crossed with them in the Charles A. Harper Company of 1855 (Church History Biographical Database; FamilySearch KWJW-43K). She married in 1864 and died at Dayton, Nevada, in the Carson Valley where many Morrisites settled, in 1868.
