The 1861–62 Roll of Membership

Names of the men and women baptized into Joseph Morris’s Church of the Firstborn at South Weber, Utah Territory, in 1861 and 1862, as printed by George S. Dove & Co. in San Francisco in May 1886.

Draft transcription. Not yet checked against the printed pamphlet. Corrections are welcome at contact@morrisite.org.

Roll of Membership: Names of Persons Baptized into the Fulness of the Gospel

USHS PAM 9569c1
Published
San Francisco: Geo. S. Dove & Co., May 1886. 8 pp. Subtitle: “Names of persons baptized during the administration of Joseph Morris, at South Weber, Utah Territory, in the years of 1861 and 1862.”
Copy
Utah History Information Center (Utah State Historical Society), Salt Lake City, PAM 9569c1; gift of William Mulder. Larson’s transcription also records the words “Reorganized Church Library,” probably a library mark on the copy.
Compiler’s note
“All the names that we found in the record book are in this list. We feel assured that there were many persons baptized whose names were not recorded.” Absence from the roll does not show that someone was not a member.
Pencil notes
Someone has totaled the names in pencil at 430 and marked the church’s officers (listed below); a further pencil note reads “174 Scan names,” apparently a count of Scandinavian names. The 429 names transcribed here fall one short of that total; the difference has not been explained.
Also printed in
Anderson, Joseph Morris and the Saga of the Morrisites Revisited (2010), 214–18.
This transcription
Made from David A. Larson’s transcription of the Utah State Historical Society copy (MORRISITES-L mailing list, RootsWeb, March 25, 2007) and spot-checked against a 2009 copy in the editor’s files, which is only partly legible. Names follow the order and spelling of the roll; where a name repeats, the roll adds “2.” Notes on doubtful readings are numbered.

Reading the roll

  • Spellings vary, especially for Danish and Swedish names: search under Hanson and Hansen, Peterson and Petersen, Christiansen and Christensen, Sorenson and Sorensen. The roll itself spells some families two ways (Peterson and Petersen; Lorensen and Lorentzen).
  • The roll records baptisms in 1861–62. It does not show who remained in the church, who left before the siege of June 1862, or who later joined the Soda Springs, Carson Valley, Walla Walla, or Montana groups.
  • Later rolls, including those of the Montana church, which existed until 1969, are cited in Repositories but not published here.
