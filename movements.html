Where they went

After the siege of June 1862 the Morrisites scattered: to Soda Springs with General Connor in 1863, to the Carson Valley and California, to the Deer Lodge Valley in Montana, and with William W. Davies to Walla Walla. This map follows the people on the roll as the sources place them, year by year.

Each dot is one person on the 1861–62 roll of membership. Everyone starts at Kington Fort in 1862; a dot moves only when a source places that person somewhere new (a census, Shupe’s list of the families who came to Soda Springs in 1863, testimony in the 1880 Davies suit, a land patent, or a death place in Family Tree), and it disappears after the year of death. 148 of 430 people are traced so far; the others drop off the map after 1862 until a source places them (tick “Show the people not yet placed” to see them waiting in a box). Press Play, drag the slider, or choose a family. Tap a dot for the person and the sources.

    How to read it. A dot moves in a straight line between two dated sources; the line is not the route taken, and the move happened at some time between the two dates. Census places are shown at the town or county; a death place known only by state is shown in the middle of that state. Places a few miles apart share a cluster (South Weber, Uintah, Plain City and Kaysville; Carson Valley, Carson City and Dayton). Places east of the map (Plano, Illinois; Missouri) are pinned to its right edge. Probable identifications are hollow, and every stop is listed below with its source. The data are in roll-paths.tsv; additions and corrections are welcome (Share records).
